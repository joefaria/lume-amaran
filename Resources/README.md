# Lume artwork

AppIcon.png is the selected original glass orbital/light-beam artwork made with the built-in image_gen tool. AppIcon.icns contains the macOS 16–1024px representations, resized with sips and packaged by iconutil. These files belong to Lume under its MIT license. The icon is flattened glass-style artwork, not an Icon Composer layered asset.

Final edit prompt:

Edit this Lume macOS app icon. Keep the centered original orbital light-beam symbol and rounded-square proportions, but replace the machined metal with Apple Liquid Glass aesthetics: optically clear smoked glass tile, polished refractive rim with a restrained thin specular edge; the orbit is smooth milky glass, the central vertical beam a clean frosted-glass elongated lozenge with a softly warm core, not a glowing flame. Remove the brushed/noisy metal texture completely and remove deep bevels. Make it elegant, futuristic and simple, a spaceflight light instrument made of glass. Obsidian translucent background with a subtle amber light reflection near the lower left and one tiny amber meeting point on the orbit. Avoid neon, glitter, stars, text, NASA logos, multiple variants, a phone, any presentation mockup, a circular planet. Crisp geometric readable silhouette at small sizes. Transparent outside the square tile; one final app icon only.

The minimal monochrome menu bar icon is a separate Phosphor planet SVG; the app icon is never rendered in the menu bar.
